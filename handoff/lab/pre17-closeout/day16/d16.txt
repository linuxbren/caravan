Caravan finally has a face: meet the web interface.

Day 16 of building Caravan in the open, my homebrew app for running AI models across my Mac minis.

Until now it ran from the terminal. Now there's a page for it:
- Chat with a model running across my Mac minis
- Topology: a live card for each Mini, showing CPU, GPU, RAM and temp
- Models: search Hugging Face, see each model's size and whether it fits, then pick where it lives: one Mini, split across two, or a copy on each
- Self-tuning that learns the fastest settings for each model

It's built for however many Macs you have. Splitting one model still tops out at 2 Minis, and so far I've only tested 1- and 3-Mac layouts with simulated setups.
