# Day 21 — Balance and Thunderbolt, 20 seconds

Live UI crops from the shipped Balance and Thunderbolt panels. One idea per
caption, all one line at the bottom. Opens with the viewer benefit: pick how
your Macs share the model, or let it decide. Apply is shown black when ready
and grey while reloading. The reload banner and per-Mini steps are the real
UI. Chat-ready time is rounded to about 13 seconds in the caption; exact
8.82 s backend Ready and 12.93 s chat-usable times are on a plain final
card. Thunderbolt shows the verified EXO1→EXO2 link from the live panel.

No dates, test counts, or reconstructed streaming. Screenshot pixels are not
rewritten. This cut does not claim post-reboot restore. Build uses only the
saved live screenshots; no Apply, Load, Unload, or new screen recording.

Build: `python3 handoff/lab/videos/make_day21_video.py`
Requires Pillow, ffmpeg, and DejaVu Sans. Output `/workspace/caravan-day21.mp4`.
Sources, captions, and measurement notes are in `manifest.json` and `captions.txt`.
